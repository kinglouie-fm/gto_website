## Identity and activities

GTO Luxembourg is a community-driven ASBL and a small, passionate group of car
enthusiasts from Luxembourg. Founded in 2017, it is built on friendship and a
shared passion for cars. The team has eight members according to the supplied
information.

GTO organises friendly automotive events, prestige car tours, and photoshoots
around exceptional cars. It appreciates all kinds of cars, with a main focus
on supercars, hypercars, and selected sportscars.

## Main events

### Supercar Sunday by GTO Luxembourg

GTO's main annual event. A free public gathering where owners and enthusiasts
meet in a relaxed, respectful, and family-friendly atmosphere around a selection
of supercars, hypercars, and selected sportscars.

Participation with a car is subject to selection because space is limited and
GTO aims for a high-quality, diverse lineup. Vehicle registration is distinct
from public attendance.

Historical 2026 details:

- Date: 21 June 2026.
- Time: 14:00–17:00.
- Location: 33, rue de la Gare, L-7535 Mersch.
- Entry: free.
- Vehicle registration channels: email, Instagram, or Facebook listed above.
- Vehicle registration deadline: 7 June 2026.
- Status: completed; registration is closed.

### GTO goes Luxembourg

A smaller, more exclusive car tour through Luxembourg. It takes place in a
private, relaxed setting, allowing participants to enjoy the drive, discover
Luxembourg roads, and connect with other car enthusiasts. Participation is free.

It is usually organised spontaneously depending on the weather, often around
August. This is a general pattern, not a confirmed date for any future edition.
The 2026 edition is completed.

Reusable French description:

> GTO goes Luxembourg est un tour plus exclusif organisé par GTO Luxembourg à
> travers le pays. L’événement se déroule dans un cadre plus privé et détendu,
> permettant aux participants de profiter de la route et d’échanger avec d’autres
> passionnés d’automobile.

## Communication preferences

- Use simple, natural, polite language. Keep texts short unless detail is needed.
- Sound like a small, passionate ASBL, not a large company.
- Avoid corporate language, exaggerated marketing claims, and a pushy sales tone.
- Be realistic about sponsor visibility; do not overpromise.
- Use French by default for sponsors, official messages, press communication,
  and most external communication.
- Use Luxembourgish for local, friendly, or informal communication when requested.
- Use English when requested. Follow the requested language and scope when
  editing an existing draft.
- WhatsApp messages should be short, natural, and friendly.
- Avoid “balade automobile”; prefer “tour”.
- Personalise outreach with a genuine connection to the business. Use placeholders
  when the connection, figures, funding purpose, or benefits are not supplied.
